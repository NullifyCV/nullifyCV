How the privacy works

Files never leave your device

pdf-lib and mammoth.js run entirely in your browser's memory. There is no server receiving your documents — not even ours.

Open source & auditable

Every line of code that handles your documents is public on GitHub. You can verify the privacy claims yourself — no trust required.

Supports data minimisation workflow

Supports data-minimisation and purpose-limitation workflows, plus internal recordkeeping for structured blind review. It does not determine legal compliance.

Separate, limited online services

Payments, licence checks, analytics and error reporting use online services. The CV redaction itself stays on your device. See the privacy policy for the full data flow.
