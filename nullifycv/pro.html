Can I anonymise 100 CVs at once?

Yes. Annual batch access costs €49 per year and supports up to 200 PDF or DOCX files per batch. Processing runs in your browser, and you can download the results as a ZIP. Review each result before sharing.

Prices in EUR, including applicable tax. Existing purchases keep their purchased terms.

Your CV stays on your device.

Stripe processes your payment. Online services handle licences, usage analytics and error reporting. We do not upload CV files for redaction. Read how data is handled.

What does one CV include?

Use all redaction settings on the same source file, correct your selections and download again. The purchase is remembered in this browser. A newly edited source document is a different file; contact support if you need help with a correction or lost access.

Can I cancel the annual subscription?

Yes. Use Manage billing in the tool or contact support. Cancelling renewal keeps access through the paid period. No refund or statutory consumer right is excluded by this statement.

Can I move access to another browser?

Not automatically yet. Keep your Stripe receipt. If browser storage is cleared or you change devices, email support@nullifycv.com so we can verify the purchase and explain the available options. Never send us your CV for payment support.

Does the tool find every identifier?

No. Automatic detection can miss details or select text you want to retain. Review every exported document before sharing. Workflow logs are not compliance certificates.
