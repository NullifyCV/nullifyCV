What is a blind CV?

A blind CV is a version of a candidate's CV with identifying details removed before a client or hiring panel sees it. It can support an initial skills-focused review and keeps direct contact details out of an early client submission.

Client submission

Share relevant experience and skills while holding back contact details until the next step is agreed.

Blind review

Remove selected personal details before an internal reviewer assesses a candidate profile.

Local processing

Process supported PDF and DOCX files in your browser. Your CV is not uploaded for redaction.

Details to review before sharing

Every recruitment workflow is different. Review the document and choose the details that do not belong in the first version sent to a client.

Contact details: name, email, phone number, address and profile links.
Identity signals: date of birth, nationality, location and selected pronouns.
Background signals: school names and graduation years when relevant to your process.
Document data: check file metadata and the final file name before you send it.

NullifyCV helps you select and redact details. It does not determine legal compliance or guarantee that every identifier has been found.
